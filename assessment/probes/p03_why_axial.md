# Probe p03 — why Axial?  (domain · step 2 · skill: plate_tectonics_ooi)

## Prompt (show to student)
In **2–3 sentences**: why is there a volcano at Axial Seamount, and why did OOI build a cabled observatory there?

## Answer key
- Axial sits on the **Juan de Fuca spreading ridge** *and* over a persistent magma supply (hotspot-like) — plate spreading + magma make it the **most volcanically active site in the NE Pacific**.
- OOI instrumented it because it **erupts frequently** (e.g. 2011, 2015) and can be **cabled to shore**, giving continuous, real-time data on an active submarine volcano.

## Hint ladder
- **R1:** What kind of plate boundary is the Juan de Fuca ridge, and what happens to magma at a spreading center?
- **R2:** Spreading → magma rises → volcanism; Axial also erupts every few years. Why would scientists want live wires on a volcano that active?
- **R3 (reveal):** the key answer above.

- `partial`: only one half (the geology *or* the monitoring motive).
- `wrong`: neither.
